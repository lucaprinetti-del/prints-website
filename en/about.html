About

A small firm, built on purpose.

Prinbas GmbH stays small because that's what makes SAP work well: short paths, real accountability, and a consultant who actually knows your system.

Our story

Prinbas GmbH was founded in Ticino by Matteo Prinetti, an SAP consultant who had spent enough years inside larger firms to know exactly what he wanted to avoid: layered hand-offs, account managers who never touch the system, and clients who never speak to the person doing the actual work.

Today, Prinbas remains intentionally small — one lead consultant and two assistants — so that every client gets direct access to the person responsible for their SAP environment, from the first scoping conversation through years of ongoing support.

Why a boutique firm

Large SAP implementations don't fail because of bad technology. They fail because of distance — between the people who decide, the people who build, and the people who maintain. A three-person firm removes that distance by design.

Every engagement is led personally by Matteo Prinetti, supported by two assistants who know the codebase and the client as well as he does. Nothing gets lost between departments, because there are no departments.
