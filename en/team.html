Team

The whole team, in one place.

Prinbas GmbH is intentionally small: one lead consultant and two assistants, all directly involved in every client engagement.

MP
Founder & Lead SAP Consultant

Matteo Prinetti

Matteo founded Prinbas GmbH in Ticino after years of SAP consulting within larger organizations, with the goal of giving clients direct access to the person actually responsible for their system. He leads every engagement personally, from initial scoping through long-term support, and is the primary point of contact for Prinbas' ongoing work with Migros and UBS.
